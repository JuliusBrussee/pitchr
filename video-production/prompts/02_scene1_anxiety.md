# Scene 1: THE ANXIETY — Kling 3.0 Pro Prompt

## Settings
- **Model:** Kling 3.0 Pro (NOT Turbo for final render)
- **Duration:** 8 seconds
- **Aspect Ratio:** 9:16 (1080x1920)
- **Reference:** Upload `founder_master_reference.png` as Universal Reference

## Prompt

```
A 28-year-old South Asian male founder sits alone at a wooden desk in a dimly lit
studio apartment bedroom at night. He has an oval face, warm medium-brown skin,
clean-shaven with faint stubble along his jawline, dark brown eyes, thick straight
eyebrows, short black side-parted hair slightly tousled, and a small dark mole on
the right side of his neck below his ear. He wears a charcoal heather cotton hoodie
with a small coral-orange embroidered mark on the left chest. A silver minimalist
watch with a black leather strap is on his left wrist.

The room is dark except for two light sources: a brass gooseneck desk lamp with a
warm Edison bulb casting golden 2700K light from the left side, illuminating the
left half of his face and the desk surface. A 14-inch MacBook Pro screen directly
in front of him casts cool blue-white 6500K light on the right side of his face
and chin.

On the desk: the MacBook showing a blurred text document, three crumpled yellow
sticky notes to the left, a white ceramic mug half-full with dark coffee on the
right edge, a black iPhone face-down next to the mug, and a small stack of
business cards held with a binder clip.

Behind him in soft focus: an exposed brick wall with warm terracotta tones, a
bookshelf with books and a small potted succulent.

Beat 1 (0-2s): He sits motionless, staring at the laptop, lips pressed together,
jaw slightly clenched, brow furrowed with a 2mm crease between eyebrows. His
hands rest on the desk with fingers interlaced.

Beat 2 (2-4s): His lips begin moving silently, mouthing words — rehearsing. No
sound. Eyes fixed on screen. His right thumb unconsciously rubs against his left
index finger.

Beat 3 (4-6s): He closes his eyes for 1.5 seconds. Takes a slow deep breath —
chest rises and falls visibly under the hoodie. Eyes reopen with a flash of
determination.

Beat 4 (6-8s): He exhales slowly through his nose. Jaw unclenches. He lifts his
right hand and runs it through his hair once — a self-soothing gesture. His gaze
drops to the sticky notes, then returns to the screen.

Camera: 85mm prime lens, f/1.4, extremely shallow depth of field — only his face
and the desk lamp are in sharp focus, everything else falls into creamy bokeh.
Slow steady dolly-in from medium close-up (chest up) to tight close-up (forehead
to chin) — approximately 18 inches of forward movement over 8 seconds at constant
speed. Eye level, straight-on, very slight 2-degree upward tilt. Tripod-smooth,
no handheld shake.

Style: Desaturated warm tones. Navy-blue shadows (not pure black). Amber-gold
highlights from desk lamp. Lifted blacks (IRE 5-8). Subtle 35mm film grain.
Medium-low contrast. Documentary realism. Cinematic 24fps.

Negative: jittery motion, morphing, flickering, frame inconsistency, AI smoothing,
overlit room, flash photography, neon colors, smiling, talking aloud, bright room,
static camera.
```

## Prototype First

Run this prompt with **Kling Turbo** first to test. Check for:
- [ ] Character face matches reference
- [ ] Dolly-in motion is smooth and constant
- [ ] Lighting has warm/cool split (lamp vs screen)
- [ ] Micro-expressions read correctly (anxiety → determination)
- [ ] Mole visible on neck
- [ ] No morphing or flickering

If prototype passes, re-render with **Kling 3.0 Pro** for final quality.
