## Why Sora 2 Pro for This Scene
- Best temporal coherence for complex multi-element scenes (stage + audience + character + lighting)
- 4D spatiotemporal consistency ensures lighting/clothing stay perfect across the crane move
- Superior physics for the crane-up camera movement
- Better lens flare rendering than Kling

- **Model:** sora-2-pro
