## Settings

## Prompt

```
Same 28-year-old South Asian male founder from the previous scene. Oval face, warm
medium-brown skin, faint stubble, dark brown eyes, thick straight eyebrows, short
black side-parted hair slightly tousled, small dark mole on right side of neck
below ear. Charcoal heather hoodie with coral-orange embroidered mark on left chest.
Dark indigo slim-fit jeans. White sneakers. Silver watch on left wrist with black
leather strap.

He stands in a modern open-plan co-working space. Floor-to-ceiling glass windows on
the left side let in abundant soft overcast daylight (6500K diffused light, no harsh
shadows). Light grey polished concrete floor. White desks with monitors visible 15
feet behind him, blurred. A green pothos plant hangs from a macrame planter in the
background. Two to three other people work at desks in the background, blurred and
anonymous — they never look up.

An iPhone 15 Pro is mounted on a small black Joby-style tabletop tripod at eye level,
positioned on a white desk 4 feet in front of him. The phone screen faces him showing
a red recording indicator dot.

Beat 1 (0-2s): He stands with feet shoulder-width apart, takes a breath, squares his
shoulders. He begins speaking with forced confidence. His chin lifts slightly. His
right hand comes up in a rehearsed pointing-forward gesture.

Beat 2 (2-5s): Mid-sentence, he stumbles on a word. His right hand freezes mid-gesture.
His eyes break from the camera and dart down-left for half a second. He audibly says
"um..." His shoulders drop one inch. His left hand comes up and touches the back of
his neck — an anxiety gesture.

Beat 3 (5-7s): He stops speaking entirely. Closes his mouth. Looks down at the floor
for 1.5 seconds. His jaw works slightly with frustration. He exhales audibly through
his nose.

Beat 4 (7-10s): He looks back up at the phone camera. Takes a fresh breath. In a
quieter, less confident voice he says "Okay, let me start over." He straightens his
posture again but the confidence is thinner now.

Beat 5 (10-12s): He begins speaking again to the camera. His gestures are smaller,
more tentative this time. He speaks but we see he is still uncertain.

Camera: 50mm prime lens, f/2.0, medium shot — head to waist, centered framing with
20% headroom. The phone-on-tripod is visible at the bottom-right edge of frame.
Subtle handheld micro-shake — 1-2mm of organic documentary movement, the frame
breathes slightly. Eye level, straight-on.

Audio: Office ambience — keyboard clicks in distance, muted conversation murmur, HVAC
hum. The founder's voice has slight room reverb. He stumbles and says "um" audibly.
Then says "okay, let me start over" in a quieter voice.

Style: Warm naturalistic tones. Slightly overexposed highlights from window light
(+0.5 stop). Accurate warm skin tones. Soft blown-out window light wrapping around
his shoulder creating natural rim light. Medium contrast. Very subtle film grain
(lighter than Scene 1). Documentary authenticity. 24fps cinematic.

Negative: static camera, steady cam, jittery motion, morphing, flickering, AI
smoothing, perfect polished delivery, no stumbling, studio lighting, dark room,
night time.
```
