# Music — Suno v4 Prompt

## Generate in Suno v4 (suno.com)

### Settings
- **Mode:** Custom
- **Duration:** 70 seconds
- **Style:** Instrumental only
- **No vocals**

### Prompt

```
Cinematic ambient instrumental soundtrack, 70 seconds total duration. Minimal,
warm, aspirational.

Structure:
- 0:00-0:08: Single sustained low C2 piano note with sustain pedal held, barely
  audible (ppp dynamics). The note hangs in space with natural piano decay and
  subtle room reverb.
- 0:08-0:20: Add a warm sustained viola pad playing a C3 note (pp dynamics).
  The viola tone is breathy and intimate, not bright or aggressive. The piano
  continues underneath.
- 0:20-0:36: Add two ascending piano notes (C4 then E4) played as slow whole
  notes with 2-second reverb tails. These notes repeat every 8 seconds. The
  viola pad subtly shifts to an Am chord (A2-C3-E3).
- 0:36-0:50: Introduce a very soft muted kick drum at 80bpm — more felt in the
  chest than heard with the ears. The kick should be round, deep (sub-60Hz), and
  heavily compressed. Piano becomes a repeating ascending 4-note figure:
  C4-E4-G4-C5, played as quarter notes at 80bpm. A warm synthesizer pad enters,
  playing sustained C major harmony.
- 0:50-1:00: The energy swells. Strings swell to mezzo-forte — add a second viola
  and a cello playing a sustained C major chord (C2-E3-G3-C4). The piano figure
  continues. A resolved, arrival feeling — warm and triumphant but not bombastic.
- 1:00-1:10: Everything resolves. Piano plays a single sustained C3 chord (C3-E3-G3)
  with sustain pedal. All other elements fade over 3 seconds. The piano chord rings
  and naturally decays. Last 4 seconds are silence.

No guitars. No drums except the subtle kick (0:36-1:00). No vocals. No synth leads.
No percussion fills or crashes. Keep it minimal and spacious — every note should
have room to breathe.

Mood reference: Ólafur Arnalds "Near Light" meets Tycho "A Walk" meets Nils Frahm
"Says." Warm, contemplative, building to a quiet resolve.
```

### If Suno doesn't produce the right feel, try these alternative prompts:

**Version B (simpler):**
```
Minimal cinematic piano and strings, 70 seconds. Starts with a single quiet piano
note, gradually builds with warm viola and soft muted kick drum at 80bpm. Swells to
a gentle climax at 50 seconds with strings and resolved piano chord, then fades to
silence. No vocals, no guitar, no loud percussion. Warm, aspirational, intimate.
Like a Nils Frahm piece for a tech startup commercial.
```

**Version C (genre tags):**
```
Genre: ambient cinematic neo-classical
Mood: warm, aspirational, contemplative, building to quiet triumph
Instruments: piano, viola, cello, soft muted kick drum, warm synth pad
Tempo: 80bpm
Key: C major
Duration: 70 seconds
Structure: quiet intro → gradual build → gentle climax → resolve to silence
No vocals
```

### Selection Criteria
- [ ] Starts quiet enough that it doesn't compete with Scene 1's silence
- [ ] The kick drum at 0:36 is subtle — more felt than heard
- [ ] The swell at 0:50 feels triumphant but not bombastic
- [ ] Resolution at 1:00 is satisfying — feels like an arrival
- [ ] Last seconds are silence or near-silence
- [ ] No unexpected instruments or vocal elements
- [ ] Warm, not cold. Intimate, not epic.
