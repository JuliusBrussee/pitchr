How It Works

Three steps to a
stronger pitch.

Record a live pitch or paste your script. Our AI scores you against the same rubric real investors use.

Record or Paste

Hit record and deliver your pitch live with real-time transcription, or paste your written script. Upload your deck for context-aware analysis.

Get Scored

AI analyzes your pitch across 5 categories — Structure, Clarity, Evidence, Market, and Delivery — giving you a score out of 100.

Ship Investor-Ready

Apply ranked fixes, use the AI-rewritten script, and iterate until you hit the Investor-Ready band. Track progress over time.
