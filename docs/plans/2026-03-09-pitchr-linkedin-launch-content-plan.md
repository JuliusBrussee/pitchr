## 2) Recommended fixes and improvements

### Keep
- 5-day cadence and anti-redundancy approach.
- Score-first framing with concrete actions (structure, clarity, evidence, market, delivery).
- Comment-first engagement style (close every post with a specific question).

### Add
- Named operators:
  - Owner (who drafts), Approver (who greenlights), Backup (who publishes if owner is blocked).
- Explicit **launch go-live gate**:
  - No Friday “launch live” content goes live until launch-readiness evidence is visible for launch (task 14 artifacts in `docs/plans/2026-03-09-prelaunch-launch-readiness-plan.md`).
- Cross-account coordination matrix (business page + 4 founder accounts).
- Instagram story+feed plan tied to LinkedIn themes.
- Contingency playbooks for:
  - delayed launch readiness
  - blocked founder posting
  - comment spike during first 2 hours of launch day.

### Change
- Shift from a “LinkedIn-only” view to a channel workflow (business LinkedIn + founder LinkedIn + business Instagram).
- Add hard stop checks before publishing each day:
  - post draft done before 11:00 CEST previous evening
  - one approver sign-off
  - final backup ready.

### Remove
- Unnamed-owner ambiguity (the previous plan lacks clear owners and backups).
- Unscoped assumptions (especially around the exact founder-account cadence).
- Launch posts that can be published before launch-readiness gates are green.

## 4) Business LinkedIn workflow (each day)

### 2026-03-10 (Tuesday) — Decision gap and measurable claims
- Owner: Social lead (primary)
- Approver: Founding ops
- Backup: Another founder if primary blocked
- Publish time: 09:00
- Purpose: open with evidence gap, move founder thinking from “sounds good” to “decidable”.
- Post script:
  - Great openings get attention.
  - The real stall happens after attention.
  - Founders often have:
    - pain clear, no measurable cost
    - vision clear, no urgency evidence
    - ask clear, no decision signal
  - Pitchr shows exactly what decision signal is missing.
  - Invite:
    - “What line in your pitch has the biggest evidence gap right now?”
  - CTA: `https://pitchr.live/waitlist`
- Engagement action: ask and answer top 10 replies by 11:30; offer one-line rewrite to commenters who share their opening sentence.

### 2026-03-11 (Wednesday) — Make claims testable
- Owner: Social lead
- Approver: Content owner
- Backup: Founding ops
- Publish time: 09:00
- Purpose: replace confident-sounding claims with testable statements.
- Post script:
  - “If your line sounds confident but cannot be tested, it still feels random.”
  - Example: weak claim vs structured claim with metric/unit/timeframe.
  - Rule for founders: “one number, one unit, one time frame.”
- Engagement action: pin 1 best example rewrite in comments and respond with one follow-up question to each top 8 commenters.

### 2026-03-12 (Thursday) — One day to launch credibility update
- Owner: Social lead
- Approver: Founder lead
- Backup: Another founder
- Publish time: 09:00
- Purpose: build trust through transparent product improvements before launch.
- Post script:
  - “One day to launch. No hype. Here is what changed...”
  - List concrete improvements.
  - Ask for deadline: “Do you have the top 1-3 fixes?”
- Engagement action: respond to each founder-typed comment with a targeted improvement angle (structure/evidence/market/delivery).

### 2026-03-13 (Friday) — Launch day execution
- Owner: Founder lead for business page
- Approver: CEO
- Backup: Operations + one founder if needed
- Publish time: 08:00 and pinned 11:00.
- Hard gate before publishing:
  - Task 14 verification artifacts are posted in launch thread.
  - No unresolved P0/P1 item marked “must fix before launch” in launch board.
- Post script:
  - “Unlucky 13 is superstition. Prepared 13 is launch execution.”
  - Product outcomes:
    - score in five buckets
    - ranked top 3 fixes
    - rewrite to test
    - run-by-run improvement tracking
  - Ask: “What will you improve first: structure, evidence, or the ask?”
- Engagement action:
  - Launch window response team at 08:00–10:00.
  - First 90 minutes: reply every relevant thread with one practical move.
  - Move qualified interest to waitlist DMs.

## 6) Business Instagram workflow (Tue–Fri)

Instagram is a repurpose channel for business LinkedIn themes.

### Tue 2026-03-10 — Claim proofing
- Feed: 4-slide carousel (weak claim vs rewritten claim + call to action)
- Stories: “Proof Check” template
  - Poll: no number / one number / number + timeframe
- Caption style: hook, one principle, two bullets, one question.
- CTA: “Drop your first two lines and one metric you can defend.”
- Time: feed 08:45, stories 09:30.
- Response: comment within 30 minutes, DMs for full teardown requests.

### Wed 2026-03-11 — Teardown challenge
- Feed: quote card + 3-slide correction carousel.
- Stories: “Anonymous teardown” 4-card sequence.
- Caption style: problem → teardown points → one rewritten line.
- CTA: “Drop one sentence for a rewrite in comments.”
- Time: feed 12:00, stories 12:30 and 20:00.
- Response: first 10 rewrite requests within 45 minutes, unresolved by end of day.

### Thu 2026-03-12 — Launch readiness
- Feed: 5-slide checklist format showing reliability/product updates.
- Stories: “Launch readiness ladder”
- Caption style: concise updates + one reflective question.
- CTA: “Comment your deadline so we can point to your highest-impact fix.”
- Time: feed 09:00, stories 09:20 and 18:00.
- Response: reply all comments by 11:00 and save recurring objections to content notes.

### Fri 2026-03-13 — Launch day
- Feed: 4-slide value stack + pinned short launch post.
- Stories: “Launch loop” with result stack and poll.
- Caption style: execution-first, low hype, high clarity.
- CTA: “Get first-access and a rewrite route on launch day.”
- Time: feed 08:00, stories 08:15 and 17:30.
- Response: first 90 minutes heavy reply mode, pin top 3 comments.

## 7) Operational notes and success checks
- Launch communication dependency:
  - if launch readiness is delayed, switch Friday posts to “quality update + revised launch timing” and remove “we launch now” lines.
- Daily KPI targets (minimum):
  - comments per post, saves/shares, profile visits, and waitlist CTR.
- End-of-day wrap:
  - top 3 objections
  - best founder replies
  - post-launch action list for next day.
