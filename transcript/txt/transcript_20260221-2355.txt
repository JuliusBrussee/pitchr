Hi everyone, we are Pitcher. We basically are building an AI platform that helps people pitch better in general, like for startups, hackathons, investors. Basically, any situation where you need to be present. The idea is that pitching is really hard and people don't always get time to practice. And feedback is inconsistent because different people say different things. So Pitcher solves that by using AI to analyze your pitch and gives you feedback like scores and stuff. We are planning to integrate a lot of advanced things like multiple models and video analysis, body language, slides too.
