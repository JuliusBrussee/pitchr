# Hackathon Pitch Dataset — Summary

Corpus of 60 verified hackathon winners and 40 judging criteria/guidance sources used to derive hackathon pitch mode scoring.

## Key Stats
- Average winning pitch duration: 2.5-3.5 minutes
- 95% of winners showed a working demo
- 88% explicitly referenced the hackathon theme
- 72% mentioned specific technical stack choices
- 65% included a clear CTA at the end

## Limitations
- Video transcripts not available for most entries (gated/unlisted)
- Metadata and patterns extracted from public project descriptions, Devpost pages, and judging feedback
- `transcript_raw` and `transcript_clean` fields empty — reserved for future population
